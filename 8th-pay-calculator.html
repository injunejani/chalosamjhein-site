7th Pay Commission mein bhi yahi hua tha: naya basic = purana basic × 2.57, aur DA 1 January 2016 se phir 0% se shuru hua [2][4]. Isliye "2.57 guna salary" sahi nahi, asli farak upar wala hai.
Zaroori: ye sirf andaza hai, final number nahi
Ye calculator sirf samajhne ke liye ek reference hai. 8th Pay Commission ki report aur sarkari order abhi nahi aaye. Asli salary ya pension sirf sarkar ke official notification aur pay matrix se tay hogi. Is hisaab mein galti ho sakti hai ya niyam badal sakte hain. Koi bhi faisla (loan, kharcha, planning) official order dekh ke hi lein. Is page ke number ke aadhar pe liye gaye kisi faisle ki zimmedaari Chalo Samjhein with Nitin ki nahi hai.
Ye calculator kya nahi jodta
HRA, TA, tax, aur pay matrix ki rounding. Asli salary sarkari pay matrix se tay hogi, isliye rupaye thode alag ho sakte hain.
